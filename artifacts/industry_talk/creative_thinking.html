Introduction

On November 3rd 2026, I attended to Industry Talk program, Creative Thinking that held on UTM Johor Bahru at 10.00 AM until 6.00 PM. The speaker, Dr Zahari Othman, The Director Center for Teaching Thinking and Innovation (CenT-TI) came to UTM Johor Bahru to present all Data Engineering students about Creative Thinking. The program aimed students to introduce them about how to thinking creatively and critically during solving the program, and Higher Order Thinking Skills, HOTS.


Creative thinking is very important for industry because we need to thinking to solve the problem. During the speaker, He said that there are 4 thinking which are critical thinking, creative thinking, mathematic thinking and science thinking. But, we focusing on 2 important thinking which is critical thinking and creative thinking. Critical thinking is the method of thinking to analyse the situation or problem, reflect from the situation, and evaluate the solution to solve the problem. Critical thinking also important to promote creative thinking because it can generate an alternative solution, getting the perspetive of the solution, and predict the outcome from the solution. He said that in industry, the companies want all of the employeed must thinking critically and creatively to solve the problem and avoid it from disasterous of the companies that let the employeed finally fired from it.
